## The line

> I told HR I was burned out; they gave me a stress ball and a deadline.

One sentence, 70 characters. You confess burnout to HR and the "help"
you get is a stress ball — the token wellness gesture — plus a deadline,
which is what burned you out in the first place. The cure is the cause.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) assembles the line from
its setup and punchline parts, validates it against the bounty gate
(UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line), and writes
it to `/output/entry.txt`.
